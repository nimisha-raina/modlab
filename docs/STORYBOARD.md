# Storyboard: electricity makes magnetism

Class 8, India. The source storyboard is 48 seconds at 24 fps. Measured
Indian-English narration stretches these sections to the final 82.08-second
lesson; the times below are source times, before retiming. See
`assets/narration/narration-timing.json` for the final segment boundaries.
One continuous camera, with the microscopic teaching model anchored in the wire.

| Time | Visual and teaching point |
| --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Circuit visible from frame 1; zoom begins at 0.5 s. |
| 3–6 s | Camera approaches the actual wire; its cover fades into a cutaway. |
| 6–9 s | Compact copper ions vibrate. Sparse blue markers move randomly. Switch open. |
| 9–15 s | Switch closes. Clearly labelled left-to-right electron drift begins. |
| 15–21 s | Follow markers entering from the negative-side wire and exiting towards positive. |
| 21–27 s | Reveal three concentric green circles with direction arrows; camera turns gently. |
| 27–37 s | Slow, continuous pullback. The cutaway closes as the original circuit view returns. |
| 37–42 s | Camera gradually widens and turns to make the circular field guides readable. |
| 38–44 s | Distinguish electron drift (- to +) from conventional current (+ to -). |
| 44–48 s | Switch opens, current and field guides stop; end with the next question. |

The green guides are revealed after current is established as a teaching step;
the actual magnetic field is present from the start of current, not delayed by
12 seconds. Their radii are enlarged during the pullback for readability, not
to depict a wave or an increase in field strength.

Orange ions and blue electrons are representative markers. Their counts,
spacing and speeds are illustrative; the image does not quantify charge density.
The closer ion spacing is fixed before and after the switch closes. This
chapter explicitly keeps the classroom/laboratory viewpoint throughout.
