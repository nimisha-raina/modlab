These timings describe the revised Blender draft, not the bundled reference
video. Ions and electrons appear together at the completed zoom (11 s). The
large title, bottom caption panel and footer are absent. Apparatus labels remain.

Green guides begin appearing when current starts. Drawing on a guide is a
visual explanation, not delayed propagation of the field. Close-up guide radii
grow during pullback for readability and remain visible alongside distributed
overview guides. This does not depict a wave or an increase in field strength.
The old narration is a guide track; sections 6 and 8 require revised wording
before the next narrated export. See the handoff for the media distinction.

Orange ions and blue electrons are representative markers. Their counts,
spacing and speeds are illustrative; the image does not quantify charge density.
The closer ion spacing is fixed before and after the switch closes. This
chapter explicitly keeps the classroom/laboratory viewpoint throughout.
