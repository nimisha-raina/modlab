# Electromagnetism application artwork

`electromagnetism-applications.png` is a 2172×724 educational triptych, generated
with the built-in image-generation tool. The three equal panels are sampled
with Blender UV coordinates and packed into the Case 2 scene. They are conceptual
cutaways rather than photographs of actual equipment construction.

## Artwork brief

Create three equal square panels in one wide, bright, realistic textbook-style
illustration for Class 8 students. Show a scrapyard crane lifting steel, a dynamic
microphone, and an MRI scanner. Use clear equipment views and cutaways that reveal
the crane's copper lifting-electromagnet winding, the microphone's small moving
coil and permanent magnet, and schematic MRI magnet windings around the bore.
Keep the subjects recognizable and uncluttered, with neutral light backgrounds,
consistent rendering, no text, logos, watermarks or people. Leave the magnetic
parts visible so the lesson can highlight them on the laboratory board.

## Scientific context

- The crane represents a controllable lifting electromagnet.
- A dynamic microphone induces an electrical signal when sound moves its coil
  within a permanent magnet's field. It is an application of electromagnetism;
  it is not the same powered soft-iron electromagnet used in the clip experiment.
  [Shure explanation](https://service.shure.com/articles/en_US/Knowledge/difference-between-a-dynamic-and-condenser-microphone).
- MRI employs a strong magnetic field. Its cutaway illustrates the magnet's
  location around the bore, without claiming an exact manufacturer layout.
  [NIBIB overview](https://www.nibib.nih.gov/science-education/science-topics/magnetic-resonance-imaging-mri).

No external photographs or manufacturer diagrams were copied into this asset.
